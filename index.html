How to play

  • Pull back from one of your buttons and let go to flick it. The further you pull, the harder it goes.
  • Possession: you keep playing as long as your team was the last to touch the ball, up to 5 flicks (the dots under your name). If you miss it, it last touched an opponent, or your 5 touches are used, the turn passes.
  • Falta: hit an opposing button before your button reaches the ball and it is a foul. The fouled button gets a free kick with the ball at its feet and the fouling team pushed back.
  • Out of play: the felt runs on past the white lines, so buttons can go there to reach the ball. Once the ball is fully over a line it is out: over a touchline, a throw-in (lateral) to the other team; over a goal line, a corner (escanteio) if the defending team touched it last, otherwise a goal kick (tiro de meta). For the restart, your nearest button steps up behind the ball. As in football, the button that takes a throw-in or corner may not play the ball again until another button has touched it.
  • Shots: once the ball is in the opponent's half, the Declare shot button appears: press it before shooting. The other side then drags its goalkeeper into place and turns it with the round handle (or the mouse wheel). A goal from an undeclared shot does not count and gives a goal kick. Own goals always count.
  • Goalkeeper kick: on a goal kick, or whenever the ball rests in your own small area on your turn, you may pull back from the ball itself and send it upfield without using a button.
  • First to 3 goals wins. The team that concedes takes the next kick-off.
