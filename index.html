How to play

  • Pull back from one of your buttons and let go to flick it. The further you pull, the harder it goes.
  • Possession: you keep playing as long as your team was the last to touch the ball, up to 3 flicks (the dots under your name). If you miss it, it last touched an opponent, or your 3 touches are used, the turn passes.
  • Falta: hit an opposing button before your button reaches the ball and it is a foul. The fouled button gets a free kick with the ball at its feet and the fouling team pushed back.
  • Shots: press Declare shot on goal before shooting. The other side then places and tilts its goalkeeper. A goal from an undeclared shot does not count and gives a goal kick. Own goals always count.
  • Goalkeeper kick: when the ball rests in your own small area on your turn, you may pull back from the ball itself and send it upfield without using a button.
  • First to 3 goals wins. The team that concedes takes the next kick-off.
